## 2. ICMR-NIN 2024 Guidelines vs. Athletic Lifter Needs

The **Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) & National Institute of Nutrition (NIN)** recommend a baseline RDA of **$0.83\text{ g/kg}$** of body weight for a sedentary Indian adult. 

However, for strength training, hypertrophy, and resistance-trained athletes, international sports science consensus (ISSN, ACSM) dictates:

- **Muscle Hypertrophy & Strength:** $1.6 - 2.2\text{ g}$ protein per kg of body weight
- **Fat Loss & Cutting (Preserving Lean Mass):** $2.0 - 2.4\text{ g}$ protein per kg of body weight
- **Calorie Split Architecture:**
  - **Protein:** $25\% - 35\%$ of daily kcal
  - **Carbohydrates:** $45\% - 55\%$ of daily kcal (Complex carbs: Whole wheat roti, Oats, Dals, Brown/White Basmati Rice)
  - **Fats:** $20\% - 25\%$ of daily kcal (Healthy fats: Mustard oil, Cow Ghee, Almonds, Flaxseeds)
